> # **Sonic the Hedgehog**

*Sonic, a blue alien hedgehog with supersonic speed, teams up with a small-town sheriff to stop the evil Dr. Robotnik from using his powers for world domination.*

> ## **Movie Overview** 
  ### **Release Date:** *February 14, 2020*
  ### **Director:** *Jeff Fowler*
  ### **Genre:** *Action, Adventure, Comedy, Sci-Fi*

> ## **Main Characters**
  ### **Sonic**
  - A fast, witty blue alien hedgehog.
  ### **Tom Wachowski**
  - A kind-hearted small-town sheriff who helps Sonic.
  ### **Dr. Robotnik**
  - An eccentric and evil genius trying to capture Sonic.

> ## **Setting**
  ### **Green Hills, Montana**
  – A quiet rural town where Sonic hides and befriends Tom.

  ### **Various Earth locations**
  – Including cities and secret labs as Sonic and Tom go on the run from Robotnik.
